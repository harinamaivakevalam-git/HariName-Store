About Harinama Store

We are a small team with a big purpose — to bring Krishna consciousness into everyday life through simple, beautiful reminders.

Harinama Store is more than a brand. It is a movement to inspire, connect and share the timeless message of love, peace and devotion. Each keychain, japa accessory, and gift set is thoughtfully handcrafted to help you pause and remember the divine in every step of your day.

“Harinama Store. Remember Krishna. Share Krishna.”
